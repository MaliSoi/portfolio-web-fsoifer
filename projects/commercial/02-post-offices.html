Post Offices

Commercial • 1998 • Location: Buenos Aires, Argentina

Overview

For the privatized Correo Argentino, the studio developed a new open and contemporary store concept, integrating exterior signage, interior design and furniture. Diagonals and strong colors were used to achieve a dynamic look. The traditional red post box (inherited from the British Post Office) was reinterpreted and incorporated as a main feature of the new design.

After the conceptual phase, 5 prototype stores were designed and supervised during construction.
