Diagnostics Clinic B

Commercial • 1999 • Location: San Isidro, Buenos Aires Province, Argentina.

Overview

This diagnostics’ and analysis clinic, had outgrown it’s current space and needed to modernize to properly accommodate the needs of both patients and medical staff. The existing building was completely remodeled and additional square footage was added during the renovation. This corner building’s design is based on two solid masonry elements flanking a three-story atrium with a glass façade open to the corner.

The distorted geometry helps to re-center the entry away from the street corner and balance the areas on both sides of the atrium. Porphyry stone, textured stucco, metal and glass accentuate the contrast between void and solid.

Palermo University Decade Award 2009.
