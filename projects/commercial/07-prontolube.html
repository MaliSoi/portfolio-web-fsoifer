Prontolube Pennzoil

Commercial • 1996 • Location: Buenos Aires city, Argentina.

Overview

For this Pennzoil Argentina automobile oil and lubricant center we chose a design based on the imagery of the Googie architecture famous across the Route 66 and American Diner landscape. Dynamic geometries, vertical pylons, graphics incorporated to building shapes, shiny surfaces and neon, reinterpreted.
