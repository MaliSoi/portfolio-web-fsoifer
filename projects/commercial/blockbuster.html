Blockbuster

Commercial • 1994-2001 • Location: Argentina and Uruguay

Overview

When Blockbuster Video came to Argentina in the 1990’s, the studio was challenged with interpreting the company’s retail outlet standards, used in the United States and adapt them to work with Argentine and Uruguayan building standards as well as meet the unique requirements necessary to serve local customers. Renovations, free standing and on pylon prototypes were adapted to the construction types and sizes of the Argentinean and Uruguayan urban sites.

Blockbuster’s American building designs were adapted and construction was supervised for over 60 Blockbuster stores, infiltrating the market across Argentina and Uruguay.
