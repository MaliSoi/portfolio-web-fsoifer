Mount Vernon Row House

Unifamily • 2005 • Location: Washington DC, USA.

Overview

This row house renovation’s main goal was to open up the space on the main level. In order to create amplitude while creating defined spaces, a one foot curved wall was introduced as a spatial divider. The wall features irregular illuminated openings and defines the geometry of the main level.
