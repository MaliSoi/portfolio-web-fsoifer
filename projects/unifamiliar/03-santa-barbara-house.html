Santa Barbara House

Single Family • 2006 • Location:Pacheco, Buenos Aires Province, Argentina.

Overview

This project’s main goal was to create an inclusive and private house in a very open and extroverted-gated community neighborhood. In order to achieve privacy the house is organized around an enclosed front and side courtyard that includes a yoga room and a water fountain. The architectural language is based on different heights prisms and alternating rectangular openings.
