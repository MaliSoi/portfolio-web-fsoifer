Libertador y Guayrá

Multifamily • 1991 • Location: Buenos Aires city, Argentina.

Overview

In these density studies for two prominent urban blocks, the team developed a stepped massing in order to capture the maximum FAR according to the zoning setbacks. The massing treatment was inspired in the Kavanagh Building one of the most famous examples of the art deco style in Buenos Aires.
