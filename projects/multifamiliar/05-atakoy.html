Atakoy

Multifamily • 2007 • Location: Istanbul, Turkey.

Overview

In this high-end residential building overlooking the Phosphorus Sea, the project introduces the nautical themes in order to articulate the massing. The sail, the bull’s eye windows and the vertical mast juxtapose with a massive opening on the penthouse floors. The curved geometry and the colored elements bleed into the landscape and the adjacent parking structure.
