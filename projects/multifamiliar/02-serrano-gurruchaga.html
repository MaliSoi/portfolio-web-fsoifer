Serrano y Gurruchaga

Multifamiliar • 1993 • Location: Buenos Aires city, Argentina.

Overview

For these massing studies in a dense urban area, the team developed various stepped massing alternates with tops that could be recognized in the city skyline. The towers are placed in the corners trying to integrate with the cityscape by corner entrance plazas and massing that relates to the street.
