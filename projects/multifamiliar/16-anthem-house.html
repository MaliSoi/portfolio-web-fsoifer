Anthem House

Multifamily • 2014 • Location: Baltimore, MD, USA.

Overview

A gateway project for a urban infill area in Locust point, adjacent to Baltimore Harbor and Highway 95. The site was a former industrial site. The building Z shape articulates a south and a north facing courtyards, each with its own landscape character and amenities. The building sets back on the main corner and residential entry creating urban courtyards to engage with the neighborhood.

The project facades take elements and materials of industrial warehouse architecture and re combines them to achieve its own urban identity. Base, middle and top are defined by contrasting masonry; a double story gray grid and three large bronze color scaled folding planes. The folding planes are directional and highlight the main entrance, the office space penthouse and the penthouse units towards the harbor.
