Market 42

Multifamily • 2018 • Location: Charlotte, NC, USA.

Overview

This 550-unit mixed-use project will occupy a full city block facing the new streetcar line adjacent to the University of North Carolina campus in downtown Charlotte. It is a multi-component development comprising a 900-car parking garage, a 7-story residential building, a 33-story residential tower, and a 10-story office building.

To the south, the commercial podium is oriented toward a private pedestrian street running parallel to the streetcar and its new station. Glass and a strong horizontal band separate the base from the residential structure. An opening with a paseo divides the block, connects the street to the garage, and creates a counterpoint to the glazed volume of the common areas.

The residential building develops an “E”-shaped volume with courtyards open to sunlight and views. The façades are articulated with contrasting light and dark volumes, along with alternating color accents. To the north, the high-rise is an “L”-shaped extrusion, where the architecture emphasizes the volumetric composition by accentuating the difference between the wings and the central portion of the building. The wings present a solid, vertical, monochromatic expression, while the center is expressed horizontally with more vibrant and colorful patterns.

Bold cantilevers and large-scale graphics highlight the common areas at the base, throughout the development, and at the crown of the building, defining the future identity of the project within the urban skyline.
