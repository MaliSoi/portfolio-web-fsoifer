1028 Market Street

Multifamily • 2014 • Location: San Francisco, CA, USA.

Overview

The project is located in an interstitial site, where two urban grids collide and produce Market Street, one of downtown San Francisco’s main arteries. The diagonal Street produces a series of triangular or “flatiron buildings” that stand out within the rectilinear urban fabric. The void on top of the triangular lot in the intersection of Market Street and Golden Gate Avenue provides a unique opportunity to expose our building façade to the broad urban skyline and transform it into a virtual corner project.

The massing concept is organized around two main façade planes or layers, one masonry and one metallic, that interact to harness and express the unique urban energies and building heights around the site. The open corner exposes two angled elevations that resemble a hinge between planes. The angular intersection or hinge is highlighted and used as a theme to articulate the metal layer around the building. On Market Street another recessed hinge creates an urban gap where the gym box and residential lobby space emerge.
