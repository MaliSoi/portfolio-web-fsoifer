Building 202

Multifamily • 2007 • Location: Washington, DC, United States.

Overview

This massive building was originally a gun factory for the Army during WWII and is located in the newly redeveloped South East Waterfront District along the Anacostia River.

The project required the renovation of the existing heavy steel structured lateral naves and its 6-story central atrium, with the addition of a 4-story metal bay and a new 2-story roof top structure. In order to bring light and air to the apartments the project contemplates the removal of the roof while maintaining the trusses and structure.

The new metal additions are designed as gentle mirrored but displaced curves that create a contrast with the rectilinear brick façade and produce an asymmetric effect approaching the building from the south and north ends. The interior central space is treated as an open courtyard with a green plaza. The units are designed around the central space on the two side naves.
