The Monty

Multifamily • 2013 • Location: Bethesda, Maryland, USA.

Overview

This free standing 17 -story tower responds to its surroundings with a through the block passage and a series of setbacks. The architecture is articulated through three layers, a red 5-story brick base, a tan brick middle and a black brick core and top. The black core accentuates the verticality and minimizes the building impact blending glass and wall in a single texture.

The red base relates to the existing adjacent buildings. The exposed concrete sunscreens, columns and fins accentuate the building ends and the south facade.
