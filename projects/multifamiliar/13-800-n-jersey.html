Parcel 11

Multifamiliar • 2017 • Location: Washington DC, USA.

Overview

The 300-unit multi phase mixed-use project features an undulating façade facing 295 highway in the Navy Yard district. The building is articulated by the expression of its main components: retail, amenities and parking, undulating residential façade facing the highway, glass bay, top trellis and black brick component as a transition to phase 1.
