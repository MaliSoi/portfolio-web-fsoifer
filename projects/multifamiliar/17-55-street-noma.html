55 M Street- NOMA

Multifamily • 2017 • Location: Washington, DC, USA.

Overview

Located in the newly developed NoMa neighborhood, this 13-story mixed use infill project features 438 units with a large array of amenities: private courtyards and roof top and a ground floor lobby integrated to a food market. The project proposes a strong architectural juxtaposition between a brick warehouse and an angular metal and glass bay projection. The two different grids intersect gently highlighting the traditional vs contemporary duality building character. The metal bay projection, a feature allowed by DC zoning rules, resumes with an L shape fin that becomes the entrance canopy and building signature.
