Rolfe Street

Multifamily • 2017 • Location: Arlington, VA, USA.

Overview

Located in steep grade triangular block near Courthouse Metro and adjacent to Arlington Boulevard, this urban infill project proposes a densification of the block through three distinct buildings and the spaces between them: a public park and a private linear courtyard. Underground parking connects the buildings. A stepped massing high-rise building defines the urban edge towards Arlington Boulevard and scaling down towards the center of the block, a 8-story and a 5-story (Transitional Living Facility) anchor the remaining corners, carving public and urban spaces in between buildings allowing sun and vistas through the block.

The architecture uses elements of Art Deco revival according to the existing zoning regulations.
