Dormies Barrancas

Multifamily • 2010 • Location: Chapadmalal, Buenos Aires province, Argentina.

Overview

This group of 60 row houses close to the beach was designed around a common park, parking and clubhouse. The lots vary between 5mx20m and 10m x 10m contain 4 types of row houses: side by side, and front –back types. The architecture is clean and contemporary; the houses propose a leisure architecture enhancing interior-exterior spatial connection, shaded structures and private patios.
