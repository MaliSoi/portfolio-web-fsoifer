Discovery Square

Multifamiliar • 2016 • Location: Herndon, VA, USA.

Overview

Located in the Dulles Airport corridor next to a new urban neighborhood development, this 5-story project proposes a contemporary architecture of colorful frames and boxes to articulate the design.

The main façade ends gentle angle to highlight the central amenity space. The base is materialized with brick, while middle and top are build with different layers of cement panel and sidings.
