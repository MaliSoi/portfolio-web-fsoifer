View 14

Multifamiliar • 2009 • Location: 14th St., and Florida, Washington DC, USA.

Overview

A sleek solution that treats base middle and top in a contextual and contemporary fashion. The angular geometries of the glass bays respond to the views and the lot lines. Because of the volumetric articulation, it seems like a different building from each angle. The massing is geometries and materials: glass, brick, block and metal panels.

The upper sunscreen canopy defines the street geometry on the building top. The broken lines alternate with the horizontal and vertical straight elements.

The units facades are laid out like a see saw pattern to maximize the vistas towards the Capitol and the National monument.
