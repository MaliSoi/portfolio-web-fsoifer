VR3

Multifamiliar • 2015 • Location: King of Prussia, PA, USA.

Overview

This 4-story building on top of structural parking will hold a senior living facility in a new densification urban development next to an existing shopping mall.

The project proposes a multi layered contemporary architecture, with black and white frames and background and warm wood like accents in the foreground.

A large frame and canopy structure highlight the entrance and the large lobby towards the front and define the project character.
