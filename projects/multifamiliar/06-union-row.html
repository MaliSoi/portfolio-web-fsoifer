Union Row

Multifamiliar • 2007 • Location: Washington DC, USA.

Overview

Part of the new re densification of 14th corridor, next to U st and the metro station, this infill project has two different components, the tower along 14th st and the warehouses along the alley.

Recreating the space of the block internal passage, the curved geometry and metallic finishes of the warehouses new addition contrast with the brick and concrete of the historical structure.

The high rise facing 14th st is partitioned in three different buildings or components. The curved roof motif frames the center tower and adjacent alley.
