400 Army Drive

Multifamily • 2014 • Location: 400 Army Drive, Rosslyn, VA, USA.

Overview

A gateway project facing 295 highway and the pentagon. The north orientation allows an expansive glass façade with a concave geometry and concrete fin-canopy-terrace highlight the building top. Planes of black brick, red brick and glass articulate the common podium, middle and top of the perpendicular towers that will have the first row on the Rosslyn skyline.
