Parcel 11

Multifamiliar • 2017 • Location: H st. and 6th SE, Washington DC, USA.

Overview

A simple massing with a solid brick base and contrasting glass and metal structure articulate this 450 unit mixed use building in the heart of H St. revitalized corridor.

The building main facade is designed as an entity with three different but complementary components.

The sold brick base relates to H St. urban fabric, while glass and metal top articulates setbacks, corners and a mid- block tower to relate and enhance the neighborhood urban landscape.
