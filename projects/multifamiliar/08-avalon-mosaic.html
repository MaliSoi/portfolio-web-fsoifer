Avalon Mosaic

Multifamily • 2013 • Location: Mosaic District, Merrifield, Fairfax, VA, USA.

Overview

This 521-unit and 50,000 sf commercial space mixed-use building materializes the focal point of a new town center development. A public paseo and a colorful fin mark the intersection of residential and parking entrances and retail terraces. The facade is divided into different residential languages, one more public, with retail podium and one more private and vertical, to accentuate the pivots and focal point.
