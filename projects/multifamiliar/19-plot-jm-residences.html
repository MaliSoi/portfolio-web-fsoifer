Plot 1 –JM Residences

Multifamily • 2016 • Location: Pune, India.

Overview

In the fast growing suburbs around the city of Pune, India, this 12-unit 4-story project takes advantage of the local normative about double story balconies proposing an array of alternating horizontal slabs and vertical wood like planes. An L shape folded plane creates an asymmetrical frame for the overall composition. Light, dark and wood like colors and textures break up the architectural elements and sunscreens and respond to the local whether conditions.
