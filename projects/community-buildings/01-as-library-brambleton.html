Brambleton Library

Community Buildings • 2015 • Location: Brambleton, VA, United States.

Overview

This library is located in the geographical center of an urban area. The urban space proposal defines the geometry of the architecture by creating a semi-circular public plaza at the main roundabout of the development. The linear building houses a public library on the ground floor and office spaces on the upper floor. A vertical element gives the complex its civic identity and articulates the entrance.
