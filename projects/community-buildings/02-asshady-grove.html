Shady Grove Clubhouse

Community Buildings • 2015 • Location: Shady Grove, MD, United States.

Overview

This project is located at the intersection of a central block within the development. The design resolves the program along two perpendicular directions: on one hand, a linear circulation volume that connects the two adjacent streets; on the other, two volumes with sloped wooden roofs that house recreational, locker room, and administrative functions, opening toward the courtyards and the landscape.

The architecture is based on simple, contrasting elements: stone and wood walls frame the circulation, while sloped wooden roofs, columns, and glass define the functional spaces, creating a strong connection to the outdoors.
