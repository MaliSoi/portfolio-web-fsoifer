Campus Uade

Urban • 2001 • Location: City of Buenos Aires, Argentina.

Overview

In this proposal for a public urban campus competition, a new loggia defines a central cloister that unifies buildings of varying scales and architectural styles. The exterior façades play with the contrast between solid and glazed surfaces, with their volumes breaking open toward the entrances. The plaza design combines soft and hard landscaping, introducing vegetation and connecting the center of the block with the surrounding urban streetscape.

Honorable Mention, UADE Ideas Competition, 2000.
