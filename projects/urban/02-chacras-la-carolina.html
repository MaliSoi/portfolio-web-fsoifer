Chacras La Carolina

Urban • 2013 • Location: La Carolina, San Luis, Argentine.

Overview

This tourism development is located in a highly scenic area adjacent to the village of La Carolina, at an elevation of 2,000 meters in the Pancanta Hills region. The site lies within the headwaters of the Quinto River, a watershed that supplies 80% of the province’s water resources. For this reason, the preservation of the valleys played a key role in the design strategy.

The project's overarching concept is to minimize the footprint of future development in order to preserve the quality of the natural landscape. As a result, private residential lots occupy only 15% of the total site area and are dispersed throughout the landscape as Country Houses and Village Houses.

The country lots are arranged along trails that follow the most attractive views and valleys, while the village houses are concentrated in two areas: the new village center and the extension of the existing village of La Carolina.
