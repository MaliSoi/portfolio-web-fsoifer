About

Overview

Federico Soifer is a senior designer with over 25 years of experience in architecture and design.

He began his career in Buenos Aires, Argentina, working on residential and commercial projects for clients such as Blockbuster Video, BBVA Banco Francés, and Municipalidad del Pilar.

In 1991, he co-founded Cordeyro & Soifer Arquitectos, leading more than 180 projects across Argentina and Uruguay.

Since 2002, he has been based in Washington DC, contributing to over 60 projects ranging from single-family homes to large-scale urban developments.

He has worked at SK&I and Dorsky Hodgson Parrish Yue Architects, and currently serves as Design Director at KTGY Group.

Philosophy & Approach

Federico’s work focuses on the conceptual and creative phases of architecture, where ideas take form through clarity and intention.

His approach balances simplicity, elegance, and boldness, while remaining deeply connected to context, history, and place.

He embraces complexity and contradiction as essential elements of contemporary urban life, integrating them into layered and meaningful design solutions.

His work reflects a constant dialogue between innovation and tradition, creating architecture that is both grounded and forward-looking.
